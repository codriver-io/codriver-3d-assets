Inside the scene

  • Two ready-to-import GLBs: a flat Cityscape preset and a terrain preset with a fitted bridge, ground and road surface.
  • Original high and low detail models: local metres, separate from the assembled scenes.
  • Source inputs: archived elevation tiles, OpenStreetMap road facts, geographic alignment, placement records and a road profile.
  • A scene manifest and notices: coordinates, hashes, source credits and validation for each preset.

Use it in your project

Unzip the package and import cityscape.glb or world.glb into a GLB-compatible editor or engine. These compact presets require support for EXT_meshopt_compression and KHR_mesh_quantization. The frame uses metres: X east, Y up, Z south. Both presets share the origin recorded in scene.json; terrain elevations use the source DEM datum.

Experimental scene. Offline loading and exported geometry are verified. Driving physics, engine-specific imports and application integration are not yet verified. Road surfaces use the authored bridge profile; dimensions and terrain are approximations.
