# Trethewey House — Abbotsford

Trethewey House: mustard lapped siding, dark shingled attic, cross-gabled cedar roof with half-timbered cream peaks, a west bay window, two cobblestone chimneys and a south veranda with rough stone piers and tapered white posts.

Mapped house at 2313 Ware Street; carriage house, playhouse, station and garden excluded. Operator dates construction to 1919, while the heritage inventory uses 1920 and notes restoration to circa1925; these dates refer to the same retained house, not different buildings. Inventory confirms two cobblestone chimneys (one internal, one external), cross gables/dormers, half-timbered peaks, exposed rafters, thick veranda piers and bay windows. Buff brick is described for the chimney/fireplace construction; visible external cladding is cobblestone. All heights, roof pitches, window sizes, porch and stair dimensions are estimates. Original procedural face mosaics evoke fieldstone without image textures or copied artwork. South stair first tread clears grade by 0.18 m; no terrain, surrounding lawn, lettering, symbols or signs.

Silhouette and distinguishing features are authored individually. Facade windows partition wall faces rather than lying over backing polygons; far retains the silhouette and a separate coarser grid of window bays. Repeated elements are merged by material. Small decorative detail is simplified at map scale; the model is an exterior and has no interior-use claim.
