# Biltmore Hotel Tbilisi — Tbilisi

Biltmore’s modern glass tower rises in four mapped strips: three sloping roof steps lead into the full-height flat crown, with a fine blue curtain wall, strong horizontal service bands and low modern podium/glass wings.

Published heights disagree: PropertyGeorgia’s original skyscraper feature gives 138 m and 32 above-ground floors, a GTU construction paper also discusses 138 (with a unit typo in its English abstract), other summaries give 130 m and a research account gives 150 m. This model uses 138 m as an estimate pending surveyed confirmation. OSM has 36 levels and three sloping roof strips; their plan geometry and relative level ratios are retained, with facade pitch and service-band locations calibrated visually rather than treated as floor-count proof. Podium heights and glazed-link support sections are estimates. The old IMELI frontage and its external curved walkway are excluded.

## Modeling and QA

Four mapped upper strips have height functions and real sloping roofs. Shared walls are omitted below neighboring roof levels; exposed risers remain. Curtain-wall cells are clipped to sloping roof lines, with adjoining mullions and floor bands rather than layered skins. Both LODs retain every step and the open space under the short raised glass connector. Low bodies are formed from mapped part unions, keeping original footprint geometry. No logos or lettering. Both LODs preserve the named features and openings. Windows are adjoining facade partitions rather than coplanar overlays. Muted day/night palettes use the same material keys.
