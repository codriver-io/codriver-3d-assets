# Tour de l’Horloge

White waterfront memorial: four clock faces, open observation crown, tall square shaft, curtain wall and smaller turret.

Original procedural geometry authored 2026-09-27 from primary historical and photographic references. Geographic plan © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. No third-party mesh, texture or photograph is shipped.

45 m main and 12.8 m turret heights are published. Clock size, tier heights and wall are estimates. Grounded at flat quay y=0; seawall relief is omitted. Clock hands static; no interiors or Tesla hardware benchmark.

Models: CC BY 4.0. Procedural code: MIT. See [licensing](../../LICENSES.md) and [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Geographic frame

Metres; +X east, +Y up, +Z south. Geographic origin: `[-73.5457102, 45.5122339]`. Heights are visual approximations.
