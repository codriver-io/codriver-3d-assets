# Sacré-Cœur — `paris-sacre-coeur`

Original texture-free geometry commissioned for Codriver in 2026. The [basilica's description](https://www.sacre-coeur-montmartre.com/decouvrir/patrimoine-et-art-sacre/lentree-et-sa-facade/) gives the campanile as 84 m high and explains the white stone. The five dome composition, campanile, front portico and separate pedestrian terraces are recognizable features. The mapped building spans about 54 × 97 m; dome proportions, terrace widths and facade detail are visual estimates.

Origin [2.3430193, 48.8867699] is the computed centroid of [OSM way 23762981](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/23762981), retrieved 2026-09-29. Approximate 0.112 rad rotation follows the mapped north-south axis. Local y=0 is a rigid building foundation plane; the stepped terrace pieces have their own modeled levels. Axes are east/up/south metres with no DEM baked. The OSM outline bounds the building; the terraces extend beyond it. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.

Build: `pnpm build:paris-icons`. [Inspect source and GLBs](/asset-preview.html?asset=paris-sacre-coeur); measured costs are in `public/models/buildings/paris-sacre-coeur.json`. Host Cityscape: not tested here. Host Full 3D world: not tested here. Hardware timing is unmeasured.
