# Golden Gate Bridge (`golden-gate-bridge`)

The 1937 suspension bridge carrying US 101 (and CA 1) over the Golden Gate strait between the Presidio (San Francisco) and Vista Point (Marin), in its current state: six lanes with the movable median barrier, two sidewalks, International Orange. A **road-fitted bridge**: the deck, car, route, camera and HD pavement share one station/lateral/height profile built on the mapped OSM carriageways.

Files: `src/peregrine/landmarks/san-francisco/golden-gate-bridge/` (`config.js`, `footprint.js`, `geometry.js`, `views.js`, `layer.js`, `golden-gate-bridge-profile.js`, `-structure.js`, `-alignment.js` generated by `-derive.mjs`, `golden-gate-bridge.test.js`), `prototypes/assets3d/catalog.d/golden-gate-bridge.json`, exports `public/models/bridges/golden-gate-bridge{-near,-far}.glb` + `.json`. Build: `pnpm build:san-francisco-landmarks golden-gate-bridge`.

## Modelling

- **Towers** are lofted art-deco sections (notched corners and a recessed panel down every face, crisp flat faces) with the mapped setbacks as ledges; portal struts carry a grille of ribs and stepped corner brackets; saddle housings on the leg tops. The south pier stands in the elliptical fender (mapped south half mirrored).
- **Deck**: open Warren truss with verticals at 7.62 m panels on both cable planes, bottom lateral bracing, steel floor, sidewalks, kerb and outer railings (outer railing drawn solid), movable median barrier, lamp standards with heads over the roadway every 30.48 m.
- **South**: approach viaduct on steel bents, the south anchorage (mapped outline, stepped), pylon S2, the Fort Point arch, pylon S1; **north**: pylon N1, the north anchorage (mapped outline: a 22 m low block with pilaster relief and a tapered cable housing behind N1 that the cables run into; the deck truss crosses it on steel bents), pylon N2, the curved north approach on steel bents.
- **Materials** (6): `tower` and `steel` (International Orange #c0362c by day; at night the floodlit towers stay brightest and the deck steel and cables a darker red, #b0503f, still readable at 3 km), `concrete`, `asphalt` (recoloured to the map pavement), `paint`, `lamp` (amber at night).
- **Far LOD** (2-8 km): same silhouette (bounds equal to near); towers, portals, cables (5-sided, 10/5 samples), the arch; every other suspender, truss diagonals every 30.5 m without verticals, ribbons sampled every 25 m on the ramps; no lamps, paint, barrier, sidewalks or ornament.

## Flat Cityscape convention and road ownership

Cityscape has no water and no bluffs. The deck is level at 75.4 m from pylon S2 to pylon N1 and eases (smoothstep) to the loaded approach roads (`'start'`/`'end'`) over **900 m at each end**, steepest grade **12.6 %**. The ramps run over the approach viaducts and out along the mapped toll plaza / Presidio Parkway (south) and Redwood Highway (north); beyond the mapped bridge the ramp is a concrete deck on two- or three-column piers, on fill below 8 m, and pavement on grade below 0.25 m.

The two carriageways separate to 20-29 m beyond the south end, so the constant `ROAD_EDGES` (±9.45 m, the bridge's roadway) could not own them: the routed car would have dropped under the ramp. The profile therefore passes `roadEdgesAt(s)`: `ROAD_EDGES` on the bridge, each mapped carriageway's offset ±6 m beyond it (blended over 60 m), and the ramp deck is drawn on the same edges (two pavements, a bare median). This needed a **minimal shared change** (commit `6e1444d3`): `createBridgeProfile` accepts an optional `spec.roadEdgesAt(s)` used only for the authored fallback of `roadEdges()`, the sections width filter and `fittedLateral`'s authored edges. Without it nothing changes: the Montréal, Paris, Toronto (Prince Edward Viaduct) and Champlain bridge tests pass (48/48), and `golden-gate-bridge.test.js` compares profiles with and without it.

### Cityscape: verified (local app, flat, no HD lanes)

Worktree bundle `pnpm build:peregrine`, server on port 3270, Protomaps tiles. `/osm-lanes` answers 503 locally, so HD lane paint and HD lateral fitting were **not** checked; standard roads were.

- The layer loads `/models/bridges/golden-gate-bridge-{far,near}.glb`, active in both LODs (far at zoom 15.2, near at 17.2); the bridge sits on the mapped strait, towers in the water; no generic provider bridge doubles the deck and no provider tower extrusions show.
- `window.__map.landmarks.heightAt` equals the authored deck height at 16 stations on both carriageways (0, 0.43, 5.59, 25.34, 63.2, 64.14, 75.4 ... 40.07, 19.43, 3.64, 0.43, 0 m) and at **all 211 mapped carriageway vertices** with their travel headings (0 misses); null 60 m beside the ramp and null for a crossing heading.
- Both ramp feet, both directions: the deck starts at grade on the two provider carriageways with no step or gap (`probe-cityscape/foot-*-close.png`).
- Open risk with HD lanes (not testable here): `bridge-surface.js` samples the approach datum at the constant `ROAD_EDGES` centres (±4.9 m), which miss the separated south carriageways near the foot, so the south `'start'` sample may come from further along US 101. On a flat map that sample is ~0 either way.

### Full 3D world: verified with `terrainPolicy: 'absolute-deck'` (local app, Terrarium DEM)

Same bundle, terrain server on port 3272 (AWS Terrarium, z15, exaggeration k = 1), `--mode world`. The default corridor policy was tested first and rejected: one flatten zone along the whole alignment at its lowest vertex DEM (9.3 m) cut a trench through the Presidio and Marin bluffs and laid a 9.3 m strip across the strait. The bridge now uses the shared policy `'absolute-deck'` (commit `28158f46`, `bridge-layer.js`): no flattening; the group stands at baked 0 (sea level), so the towers, anchorages and the deck keep their authored heights; supports with weight 0 (approach piers and bents, pylon and anchorage feet, the tower piers' base) reach their local DEM; the ramps' `'start'`/`'end'` take the DEM at the alignment ends and a ramp never dips below the DEM sampled every 20 m along the alignment; `heightAt`, the route and the HD overlays read the same surface; unknown DEM keeps the bridge inactive.

Measured in the app (`tmp/san-francisco/golden-gate-bridge/world.mjs`): terrain footprint null, no zone, group y = 0; ramp ends at the DEM: 51.8 m (south, Presidio Parkway) and 89.7 m (north, Redwood Highway). Absolute deck = `heightAt` + DEM under the point:

## Approximations and not modelled

Railings drawn solid; suspender pairs as one ribbon; the suicide-deterrent net, wind retrofit fairing, aviation beacons, rivets, cable bands, the toll plaza and the Fort Point fort itself are not modelled; anchorage massing above ground is estimated (in Full 3D world the 22 m north anchorage block is buried in the hillside and the housing and bents rise out of it); lane paint is a fallback that the HD paint replaces. No Tesla hardware measurement.
