# Olympic Stadium

Mapped open oval stadium, brick perimeter, covered long-side grandstands and the freestanding Marathon Tower with its original flame cauldron.

Version: exterior standing on 2026-11-10, Amsterdam, Netherlands. Original procedural geometry; reference photos are comparison-only and remain in ignored scratch. No logos or lettering.

## Dimensions and frame

The mapped oval is approximately 172 by244m; the rebuilt single lower bowl, grandstand roofs15.55m and floodlights25.5m are photo estimates. Marathon Tower: RCE gives42.19m while Arcam and OSM give46m. Cauldron rim42.19m and slender mast46m are an estimated reconciliation of different top datums, not a survey.

Origin 4.85406145,52.3435074; +X east/+Y up/+Z south, y=0 local exterior grade. Footprints replace only OSM ways 57855651, 57855064, 98662844. Local flat grade at stadium entrances. Pitch and track surfaces sit at least0.10m above grade. Modern lower bowl, no demolished 1937 upper tier. Logos, Olympic rings, signs and advertising omitted. Tower balcony cantilevers are structurally continuous through its shaft. Annex rooms/seat detail and field markings are simplified.
