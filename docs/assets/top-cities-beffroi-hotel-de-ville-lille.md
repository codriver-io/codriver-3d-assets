## Modeling and approximations

The exact belfry-only base fixes ownership and bearing. Upper geometry is aligned from mapped control vertices2 and13;the long orange-brick shaft has actual adjoining pale-concrete piers and narrow dark slots. At68 m a flared concrete shoulder widens into an open projecting observation/bell head: freestanding posts contact the floor and84 m cap,while a small inner core sits several metres behind the bays. Four corner pinnacles frame a progressively narrowing three-stage open lantern and pointed roof;the final needle reaches104 m. The near and far models preserve the same main piers,real gallery air,stepped lantern,pale/brick contrast and slender profile;far mainly removes lower-panel subdivisions. Only104 m overall is treated as published;all shoulder heights,widths and profile stages are photo estimates. Two current whole-belfry exterior references were used;interiors,roof views of the town hall and rear views obscuring the crown rejected. Town hall wings,giant statues,clock faces,lettering,plaza and terrain are excluded.
