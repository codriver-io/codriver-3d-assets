# Louise Bridge (10 Street), Calgary

Original procedural model of the current 1921 Hillhurst/Louise Bridge over the Bow River. Five **closed-spandrel** concrete wall arches, four traffic lanes and pale open balustrades identify it.

Sources: [City archives](https://www.calgary.ca/info-requests/archives/building-bridges.html) establish five reinforced-concrete spans and construction in 1920–1921; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louise_Bridge) supplies 172 m length and 19.6 m width; [PCI rehabilitation article](https://www.pci.org/PCI_Docs/PCI_IllinoisWisconsin_Docs/IL_Testing/Rehabilitation_of_Historic_Hillhurst_Bridge.pdf) identifies spandrel-wall arch construction (PDF endpoint returned an HTML error locally; search excerpt corroborates construction only). Visual reference: [Calgary West End](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Calgary_West_End.jpg), Arch26, public domain; dossier `refs/1.jpg`, no image shipped. No third-party mesh or texture used.

Metric frame: +X east, +Y up, +Z south, origin `[-114.0849,51.05116]`; orientation baked from way 19164932, south to northwest (about 322 degrees). No DEM or Mercator scale baked in. Exact bridge outline way 1323522206 is retained as the inspection footprint; it does not claim adjacent buildings. Road ownership follows way 19164932 and approaches 32000911, 461139248, 4596266, 254732794; south fork uses 4676205 (9 Street outbound) and 4397264 (inbound link). The approach alignment averages the mapped fork branches; ribbons recover their individual lateral positions.

Five full-width elliptical barrels preserve actual underside voids and solid spandrels. Grounded piers and vertical exterior pilasters support the deck. Two continuous rails and merged balusters form the balustrade. Raised paths taper into narrow edge strips at connected approach pavement; rails finish at supported terminal posts at the abutments. No invented elevated towers or open spandrel columns. Reinforced-concrete texture is simplified to subdued flat material. Decorative incised surface detail and utility fittings omitted.

Cityscape uses approximately 100 m approach ramps, with a maximum measured grade below 12.5%. The two southern carriageways separate at the mapped fork, rather than extending a square 19.6 m slab across it. River deck width stays rigid; only approaches fit provider edges. World uses `bank-fit` and explicit start/bank/end controls, not riverbed control samples. This is a separate integration check, not a survey or proof of physical clearance.

Source/export verification and local app evidence are recorded below. Independent publication review remains separate.

Exported costs: near **15,712 triangles / 4 draws / 650,684 bytes (635.4 KiB)**; far **7,308 / 4 / 317,720 bytes (310.3 KiB)**. Both are well below bridge caps. Far retains all five arch openings and the full balustrade outline with half the balusters and 16 rather than 40 ellipse samples. Dense approach ribbons preserve the vertical curve (near 2 m, far 8 m). Runtime provider overlay costs are additional.

The author looked at the dossier photo and `tmp/top-cities/shots/louise-bridge-calgary/reference-export-comparison.jpg`, combining that reference with near/far, light/dark GLB overviews and a low arch detail. Also inspected the near-light sheet containing facade/back/roof/deck/underside/pier/landing views. Model silhouette agrees with the reference's shallow wall arches, vertical pier pilasters and light open railing. The geometry is simplified and clean rather than a weathered concrete reproduction. The first depth scan found trim/slab and near-grade approach material cycles: merging structural trim into concrete and extending the concrete walk 12 m beyond the slab removed all cycles. Final depth generator reports **zero dropped edges** in both LODs. The 25 mm fallback paint offset follows the bridge overlay contract so live HD pavement/paint can cover it.

Checks: initial requested two-file run passed **881/881** tests (114.6 s); final targeted run passed **7/7** after material/approach refinements. Tests raycast all five openings and solid spandrels, check deck/pier support, every baluster's named upper/lower rail contact with shortened-terminal negative control, rail ends in pier/end posts, road heading/river rejection, rigid river widths, southern fork separation, maximum grade, both GLB attachment attributes, dimensions/bounds and budgets. `pnpm assets:check`, `pnpm assets:preview`, `node scripts/asset-catalog.mjs`, depth generation and depth `--check` pass. It is not a claim of online provider coverage. Both local servers were restarted with Redis enabled for the replacement captures.

The south-in view was read: the actual provider's yellow centreline and white lane paint continue across the rigid deck and the fork joins the approach ribbons without a square slab end.

A defect in the first HD approach review required the bridge-brief shared-code exception: the shared fitter skipped every triangle below 5 cm, including ordinary approach pavement/paint buried by a landmark ramp. Separate commit **5f66a55** adds optional `profile.groundApproachAt(x,z)`. It captures only mapped approach triangles wholly inside that predicate, with a matching ground-level provider centreline and a longitudinal triangle direction; crossing and unowned roads survive. Without the predicate the prior rejection is unchanged. Louise defines the predicate from its mapped north/south branch segments and excludes the river spans. The focused shared regression set (Montréal, Paris bridges, Prince Edward, Champlain and the new ground-approach restoration/paint-phase test) passes **55/55**. Final replacement app evidence is in `app-*-after-fix`; older directories document the before state.
