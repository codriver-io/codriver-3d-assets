# Will Rogers Memorial Center — Fort Worth

1936 Will Rogers Auditorium, Pioneer Tower and Coliseum: stepped Art Deco tower with pale corner piers, matching curved-front buff-brick halls, muted mural friezes and shallow coliseum dome. Later equestrian and exhibit facilities excluded.
