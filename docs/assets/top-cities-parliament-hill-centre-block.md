# Parliament Hill Centre Block and Peace Tower, Ottawa

Original procedural permanent exterior of the 1927 Centre Block, Peace Tower and surviving Library of Parliament. Temporary restoration scaffolding, construction fences and excavation are deliberately omitted. All model surfaces are original texture-free geometry; reference photographs stay in ignored tmp.

## Dimensions and geographic frame

Official Government of Canada Peace Tower height: **92.2 m**. Mapped Peace Tower outline also says 92.2 m. The complete OSM provider envelope spans approximately 155 m including side plinths; the authored wall/trim envelope is approximately 151 m after removing the eastern foundation apron; pavilion peaks 35 m, interior towers 40–42 m and Library pinnacle 58 m follow estimated OSM part tags. Facade windows, clock diameter (4.76 m), cornices, dormers, carvings, library drum tiers and colour are photo-based estimates rather than architectural survey dimensions.

Origin `[-75.699635021875,45.42479299375]` is the centroid of the Peace Tower outline. Metres: east/up/south; y=0 is rigid local grade. Frontage axis 59.096793° and outward front normal 149.096793° are baked into vertices once. Two OSM control points `[-75.7001242,45.4247108]` and `[-75.6997600,45.4248638]` define the frontage edge (33.16 m). The opposite rear edge independently bears 238.856°, confirming the axis within 0.25°.

Provider ownership is exactly Centre Block way 68588665, Peace Tower 128127767, Library 128127770 and the 76 original nested parts plus ten entrance stair/plinth ways enumerated in footprint.js (89 owned way IDs total). Nearby West Block, East Block and western outbuilding are excluded. Eleven mapped rings are building masks, including the parent entrance stair outline and seven of its nested parts; no lawn-sized mask. Exact entrance ways added after review: 1117689261 and 1117689263–1117689271. The two tiny treads 1117689269/70 are fully enclosed by the 272 m² parent stair ring; their exact geometry is retained in ENTRANCE_PARTS and their IDs are claimed, while redundant <4 m² masks are omitted to satisfy the shared footprint-area contract. Trim and buttresses stay within 6.5 m of a mapped ring; only the broad, grade-connected entrance stairs extend up to 8.5 m beyond the tower outline. Centennial Flame way 296732958 is a separate tiny fountain at its OSM centroid `[-75.69870183,45.423728125]`, approximately 139.16 m toward the front; it does not require a building mask. The surrounding front lawn is supplied by the basemap. An explicit terrainPad contains the mapped building rings and a separate 5 m fountain ring, with three front-footing references, median datum and 8 m feather. This avoids the default 140 m disc sampling the river escarpment; its DEM fit, surrounding lattice coverage and the fountain’s relative grade still require app validation.

## Model and approximations

Mapped pavilions retain their Gothic outlines. Complete authored wing envelopes replace individual OSM half-roof parts: symmetric hipped/mansard roofs have steep lower slopes, a shallow upper pitch, a continuous ridge, solid dormers, and masonry chimneys. Front-facing roofs use subdued dark slate/copper as in the frontal photo; side and rear roofs use oxidized green copper. The two former open courts and central Hall of Honour are closed with lower copper-roofed volumes (16 m eaves, 20.5–21.5 m crowns), hidden below the main 27–30 m wing ridges from the street. Small inner turrets now have continuous masonry shafts down to the rigid foundation, rather than relying on high OSM min_height parts. Foundation edges follow actual wall volumes, removing the unsupported east apron. Original Gothic arch surfaces, a true entrance passage, four large clock faces with hands and twelve hour marks, corner buttresses/pinnacles, steep copper spire, flagpole and a geometric Canadian flag establish recognition. Separate window bays remain in far; near adds selective stone frames, mullions, buttresses and dormers. The round library has a polygonal lower drum, flying buttresses, tall upper windows, pointed gables, two conical copper roof tiers and a finial.

Eight merged materials: sandstone, dressed trim, foundation, subdued green copper, older slate/copper roof panels, glass, self-lit clock/flame/flag-white, red flag. The small flame is symbolic static geometry; province shields and accurate fire simulation are omitted. Library roof oxidation is generalized to green; fine gargoyles, carvings, tracery and individual masonry blocks are simplified. Near/far are independently simplified, with identical principal envelope. No external meshes, textures, terrain values or absolute altitude.
