# Gibeau Orange Julep

Truncated orange shell with curved service windows and canopy, recessed entrance and a separate lettered orange-slice roadside sign.

Original procedural geometry authored 2026-09-27 from operator history and public photo references. Mapped footprint © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0). No third-party mesh, font, reference photograph or texture is shipped.

18.3m sphere follows operator near-60ft account; secondary 40ft reports conflict. Mapped envelope ~21.1m. Height, facade bearing and roadside sign offset are photo estimates; flat-map grade y=0. No Tesla hardware measurements.

Models: CC BY 4.0. Procedural code: MIT. See [licensing](../../LICENSES.md) and [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
