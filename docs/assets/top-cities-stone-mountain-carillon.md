# Stone Mountain Carillon — Stone Mountain

The 117-foot lakeside carillon speaker tower: three splayed steel supports, an open triangular upper screen of vertical weathered redwood planks, suspended lower planks with a spiraling bottom edge, and visible steel girts. This is an electronic carillon speaker enclosure; exposed bronze church bells are not invented.
