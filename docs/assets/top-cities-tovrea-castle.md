# Tovrea Castle — Phoenix

Castle136668541 only; hill, garden/cacti, perimeter terrace/wall and separately restored outbuildings are excluded. NPS identifies four-storey stepped octagonal tower including the small cupola; three broad main tiers and the fourth-floor cupola are present. All heights and upper-tier setbacks are photo estimates, with only the bare flagpole2.44m length published by SAH; total15.84m is an estimate, not a sourced building-height claim. Rigid y=0 exterior foundation excludes the basement excavated into the knoll. Mapped lower outline is a clipped rectangular octagon, not a circular cylinder. Both LODs keep crenellation openings and alternating dome gores; no flag, lettering, logos, string lights or signs.

Silhouette and distinguishing features are authored individually. Facade windows partition wall faces rather than lying over backing polygons; far retains the silhouette and a separate coarser grid of window bays. Repeated elements are merged by material. Small decorative detail is simplified at map scale; the model is an exterior and has no interior-use claim.
