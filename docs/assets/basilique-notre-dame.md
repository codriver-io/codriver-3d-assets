# Basilique Notre-Dame de Montréal

Twin square Gothic towers, a recessed triple-arch portico, crenellated façade, long nave and rear Sacré-Cœur chapel complex.

Original procedural exterior authored 2026-09-27; OSM way 4320792 footprint © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0). Official/heritage photographs inform proportions only; no captured model or third-party texture is distributed.

66 m towers and 77 × 41 m nave from heritage reference; mapped overall complex about 109 m. Ground-relative flat map, no survey elevation. Roof heights, chapel massing, tracery and statues simplified; excludes temporary scaffolding and interiors. No road surface or plaza replacement. Tesla hardware unmeasured.

Models: CC BY 4.0. Procedural code: MIT. See [licensing](../../LICENSES.md) and [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
