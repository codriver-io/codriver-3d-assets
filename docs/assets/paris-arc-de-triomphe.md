# Arc de Triomphe — `paris-arc-de-triomphe`

Original texture-free geometry commissioned for Codriver in 2026. The [City of Paris](https://www.paris.fr/pages/a-la-place-de-l-arc-de-triomphe-devait-troner-un-elephant-18396) publishes 50 m height, 44.8 m frontage and 22.2 m depth. These are the exterior targets. Main and transverse portals are open, with an attic and simplified relief masses. Opening profiles, reliefs and materials are visual estimates.

Origin [2.2950373, 48.8737780] is the computed centroid of [OSM way 226413508](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/226413508), retrieved 2026-09-29. Approximate 1.0214 rad rotation follows its major axis. The [monument operator](https://www.paris-arc-de-triomphe.fr/en/content/download/9830003/file/Sales%20Guide%202025_compressed.pdf?inLanguage=eng-GB&version=45) describes the great opening as about 29 × 14 m; transverse opening and reliefs remain estimated. Local y=0 is the four-pier foundation plane; east/up/south metres, without scene or terrain elevation. The model excludes the surrounding roundabout. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.

Build: `pnpm build:paris-icons`. [Inspect source and GLBs](/asset-preview.html?asset=paris-arc-de-triomphe); measured near/far costs are in `public/models/buildings/paris-arc-de-triomphe.json`. Host Cityscape: not tested here. Host Full 3D world: not tested here. Hardware timing is unmeasured.

Visual comparison: [Centre des monuments nationaux front photograph](https://www.monuments-nationaux.fr/actualites-du-cmn/les-monuments-au-caeur-du-tour-de-france-2023). No reference pixels are redistributed.
