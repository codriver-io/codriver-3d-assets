# Saint Aubin Cathedral

Saint-Aubain Cathedral’s rounded Latin cross, red-brick and limestone walls, monumental convex classical front with twenty columns, segmental pediment and statues, and the ribbed copper crossing dome and open lantern.

Version: exterior standing on 2026-11-10, Namur, Belgium. Original procedural geometry; reference photos are comparison-only and remain in ignored scratch. No logos or lettering.
