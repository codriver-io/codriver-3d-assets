## Modeling decisions and approximations

Current exterior only. The campanile and two chapter domes retain mapped heights; small facade stage heights, bell apertures, roof ribs and grouped roof figures are estimates. Portico and upper loggia are real openings in both LODs, with recessed backing surfaces. Broad downhill rear stairs, detached obelisks/columns, plaza, mosaics, inscriptions, detailed sculptural relief and interiors are omitted. Only the basilica outline and its interior mapped parts are owned. Exterior references are photos 2 and 4; the dossier’s other large images show interiors and were rejected. Geographic yaw comes from the mapped nave edge and is baked once.
