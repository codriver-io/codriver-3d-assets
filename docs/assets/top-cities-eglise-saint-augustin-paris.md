## Modeling and approximations

The irregular mapped lower aisles and tapering nave retain the block shape while the main circular drum,turrets,ribbed elliptical slate dome and copper lantern define the skyline. Original mapped non-dome heights are scaled80/84 to reconcile the parish overall80 m description with OSM83 m spire plus cross; the diocese50 m dome description uses an ambiguous or interior datum,so these are estimates rather than a survey. The front wall has a real circular rose opening and three1.2 m deep arched portals. Clerestory and round lower windows are adjoining stone/glass partitions. Both LODs preserve the open16-post copper lantern,roof ribs,four surrounding turret silhouettes,rose and triple entrance. Original design drawings,historic doors and all interior photos were rejected;two completed exterior photos establish the current form. Fine statues and sculpture simplified,not traced; no lettering,plaza or surrounding streets.

Overall80 m follows parish and tourism operator; OSM83 m spire plus cross differs,so mapped vertical dimensions are scaled80/84 as estimates.50 m published dome measurements refer to interior cupola or ambiguous datum,not a resolved exterior survey. Original metric geometry,local ground0; no terrain or plaza.
