# Farine Five Roses — mill and silos

Brick mill, stepped industrial volumes, cylindrical silo banks, open rooftop steelwork and two correctly oriented red FARINE FIVE ROSES signs.

Original procedural geometry and original letter outlines, authored 2026-09-28. OSM-derived footprint © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0). Photos consulted for proportion only; no third-party mesh, font or texture shipped.

Heights other than the 4.572m letters, internal silo arrangement and steel member sizes are estimates. Ground datum y=0, no surveyed terrain. Static restrained red night emission; no flash cycle.

Models: CC BY 4.0. Procedural code: MIT. See [licensing](../../LICENSES.md) and [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Geographic frame

Metres; +X east, +Y up, +Z south. Geographic origin: `[-73.55135, 45.49176]`. Heights are visual approximations.
