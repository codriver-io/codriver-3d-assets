# Pont Jacques-Cartier

Green cantilever trusses, four finials, curved Montréal approaches and a raised Seaway span.

Original procedural geometry authored 2026-09-27 from public structural/photo references; mapped frame © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0). No captured mesh, third-party texture or simulator asset is shipped.

Visual approximation, not surveyed. Deck heights are adapted to the flat map; fine fabrication details are simplified. Five-lane deck; live provider lane markings take precedence. Island pavilion included with its mapped footprint and a simplified height beneath the flat-map roadway.

Models: CC BY 4.0. Procedural code: MIT. See [licensing](../../LICENSES.md) and [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Geographic frame

Metres; +X east, +Y up, +Z south. Geographic origin: `[-73.5417613, 45.5218315]`. Heights are visual approximations.
