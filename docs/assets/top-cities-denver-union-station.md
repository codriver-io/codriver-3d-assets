# Denver Union Station — Denver

Denver Union Station historic headhouse: lower pink-rhyolite hotel wings with grey hipped roofs/dormers, a pale neoclassical Great Hall with three immense arched windows, clocks, deep cornices and an open iron entrance canopy.

OSM24m central roof and14m wings are unsurveyed estimates; clock/frame crown28.2m is photo fitted, not a published height.65ft Great Hall ceiling is an interior measurement and is not adopted as exterior height. Foundation y=0 is rigid exterior grade, no terrain baked. Roof sign architecture represented only by bare iron frames: all letters/numerals/neon words, logos, flags, urn inscriptions and commercial signs omitted. Rustication and original abstract urn reliefs use procedural geometry. Current2025 front photos and2022 street view establish restored exterior. Horizontal rustication courses are face partitions, not coplanar overlays.

Silhouette and distinguishing features are authored individually. Facade windows partition wall faces rather than lying over backing polygons; far retains the silhouette and a separate coarser grid of window bays. Repeated elements are merged by material. Small decorative detail is simplified at map scale; the model is an exterior and has no interior-use claim.
