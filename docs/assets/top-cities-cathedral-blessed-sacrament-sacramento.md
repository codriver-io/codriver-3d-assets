# Cathedral of the Blessed Sacrament — Sacramento

Cathedral of the Blessed Sacrament: a tall tiered central bell tower, two lesser corner towers, French Renaissance west facade with three arched portals and rose, and a ribbed grey crossing dome with open lantern.
