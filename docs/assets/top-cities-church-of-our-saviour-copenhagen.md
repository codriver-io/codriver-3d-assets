# Church of Our Saviour — Copenhagen

Our Saviour’s Church: a steep-roofed Greek-cross brick church, square clock tower and dark copper spire encircled by a real exterior winding staircase with gold railings, globe and a small robed finial figure.

## Model and foundation

The Greek-cross body follows its detailed mapped wall outline; a single partitioned steep roof envelope removes all hidden overlaps between the two hipped arms and central pyramid. Tall round-headed windows and brick pilasters, two belfry levels and plain gold clocks establish the church at road scale. The dark tapering octagonal spire carries a true helical stair slab, individual risers, continuous gold handrail and grounded balusters. The globe, original robed finial silhouette and flag pole are distinct geometry; no insignia, lettering or borrowed sculpture mesh is used. Both LODs retain the stairs and rail negative space.

Permanent post1996 spire and existing church envelope are modeled. The owner reports facade/sandstone restoration2026–28 and temporary north-side scaffolding; temporary scaffolding is omitted. Photos2 and4 show the present permanent restored spire and church, and3 verifies the exterior stair/railing;1 is a panorama and5–6 interiors, not exterior geometry references. Globe heights follow mapped82.5–85 m; Lex prose also describes the globe at90 m but explicitly identifies90 m as flag top later in the same article. Minor trim, clocks, sculpture poses and stair radii are photo-calibrated estimates; the mapped stair angle/height progression is retained.
