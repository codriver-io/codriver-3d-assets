## Model and foundation

The main shaft is a true square in its mapped diagonal frame. Height unions remove overlapping OSM slab/glass solids; each projecting telecom deck keeps its open underside in near and far. Sparse shaft windows and board/joint rhythm, subdivided bronze-grey pod glazing and a real four-legged lattice mast supply the recognizable details. Every lattice brace and tie meets its named leg; legs rest on the concrete top and a closed headplate supports the final antenna. The low base and glass entrance follow the mapped exterior. No terrain offsets or internal rooms are included.

Existing permanent1967 concrete structure and modern operating-tower exterior. Photos3 and6 show clear tower elevations;4 verifies the low base and decks from above;1 and5 are panoramas away from the tower and2 temporary tricolour lighting, excluded.170 m total follows the owner2017 and broadcaster, while the owner2010 release’s177 m is documented as an unresolved estimate/version discrepancy rather than silently combined with the155 m concrete body. Small radio fittings, dishes, flags, signs, logos and roof equipment are omitted. Base/entrance heights, shaft joint spacing, pod frames and mast lattice subdivision are photo-calibrated estimates.
