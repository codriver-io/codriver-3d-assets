# Stockholm City Hall — Stockholm

Stockholm City Hall: the long red-brick waterfront range and true columned arcade, open Borgargården, varied copper roofs and small onion towers, anchored by the106 m square tower and open copper lantern with three golden crowns.

## Model and foundation

Height-sliced polygon unions retain the irregular whole envelope without hidden stacks. A partitioned set of gabled, hipped and sloping roof facets keeps the real roofscape; roof folds and small onion finials remain in far. The mapped waterfront columns are real separate stone shafts under an arched intrados, with open space through the colonnade. The main tower keeps a broad plain brick shaft, sparse inset windows, an open copper lantern, four original draped figure silhouettes and three individual crown baskets on supported radial arms. The court stays empty; no generic solid block fills it.

The permanent1923 building in its unscaffolded2026 state is modeled; the owner schedules tower restoration for early2027–2028. Checked photos1 and3 show court and arcades,2 is a February2026 full waterfront exterior;4 is decorative projection lighting and5–6 interiors, not geometry references. OSM heights are estimates except the city-published106 m total. Roof profiles, sparse shaft windows, lantern proportions, sculpture poses,2.2 m crown spans and arch intrados are photo-calibrated estimates. No waterfront paving, sea surface, stairs beyond the owned envelope, heraldic lettering, copied statues or flags are exported.
