## Modeling and approximations

Two narrower mechanical/water-tank stages and short aerials reach144 m;the source120 m shaft versus144 m overall describes different extents,not a scale conversion. The separate mapped four-level podium is clipped around the tower rather than stacked over it. y=0 is the rigid Place Bretagne entry datum;no lower-street ramp,retaining topography or road is baked. Two actual tower exterior photos were used;the erroneous La Defense view and panorama taken FROM the tower were rejected.
