# Samuel-De Champlain

Three decks over the Saint Lawrence, carried by inclined twin piers and open W-shaped caps.

Original procedural geometry; mapped alignment © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Supplied Flight Simulator archives were inspected as references only; no converted geometry or textures are included.

Approximate elevations and structural details; not surveyed. Both LODs checked in the app; Tesla hardware performance remains unmeasured.

Models: CC BY 4.0. Procedural code: MIT. See [licensing](../../LICENSES.md) and [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Geographic frame

Metres; +X east, +Y up, +Z south. Geographic origin: `[-73.5057186, 45.4683481]`. Heights are visual approximations.
