## Modeling and approximations

The source is assembled from literal mapped building-part rings,including their noncircular transitions and courtyard gaps,rather than a full disc. The68 m utility tower,31 m office arc,20 m lower wing,24 m inner studios and15 m auditorium volumes remain distinct. Four raised courtyard links retain open air beneath them and have physical endpoint-contact tests against separately generated named ring parts,including shortened-end negative controls. Body windows have narrow repeated rows and a continuous taller top belt; far groups bays and alternate rows. Invalid Commons top-ups (Maison des Canaux,auditorium interior and Berlin-wall fragment) were rejected; the Zairon aerial and GP SNCF riverfront photograph show the actual exterior. The GP SNCF photograph is all rights reserved and comparison-only.
