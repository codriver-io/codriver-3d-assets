# Cathédrale Notre-Dame de Strasbourg — Strasbourg

Asymmetric pink-sandstone cathedral with a broad pierced west screen, 13.6 m rose, three Gothic portals, a single north octagonal belfry and openwork 142.11 m spire, short copper-roofed nave and eastern crossing tower.
