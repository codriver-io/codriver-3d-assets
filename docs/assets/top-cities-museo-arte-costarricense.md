# Museo de Arte Costarricense — San José

Original Museo de Arte Costarricense in the former La Sabana airport terminal: white colonial wings, low tiled hips, a glazed control cabin with open iron balcony, large flattened arrival arch and two-storey northern pavilion.

## Modeling decisions and approximations

Models the current museum architecture after the2010restoration, not an operating airport. Placement and rear control-tower projection follow the mapped footprint; all vertical dimensions are explicit estimates from exterior proportions because no reliable surveyed height was found. White stucco, terracotta hips, actual recessed arch glazing and iron balcony rhythm remain in far. A narrow authored stair/landing projection is registered separately.
