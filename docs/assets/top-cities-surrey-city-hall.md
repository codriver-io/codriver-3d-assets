Roof top is a two-plane fold peaking at 28.8 m, with a continuous cedar soffit and clerestory reaching its underside. Right wing is supported by a grounded service core; the open west overhang survives in far. Frit is simplified to original opaque/clear face partitions, not photo textures. No logos, lettering, heraldry, neighbouring library, broad plaza or terrain. Atrium foundation top is 0.18 m above local grade. Roof outline extends about 4.3 m beyond the north facade projections and 3–4 m beyond end walls, a photograph-based eave estimate beyond the mapped roof-part polygon; building foundations retain exact mapped outlines. Atrium glass is separately muted blue; no civic lettering or logos are included.

Silhouette and distinguishing features are authored individually. Facade windows partition wall faces rather than lying over backing polygons; far retains the silhouette and a separate coarser grid of window bays. Repeated elements are merged by material. Small decorative detail is simplified at map scale; the model is an exterior and has no interior-use claim.
