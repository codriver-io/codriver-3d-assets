## Modelling and limits

Two acoustically independent halls are enclosed in separate architectural shells, linked by a tent-like glazed porch facing south, as documented by Safdie and the owner. Exterior2/3show the stepped/fanned metal shells and5shows the southern glassfront;1/4/6are interiors and are not exterior massing sources. Original procedural surfaces use those mapped limits, swept curved roofs, physical overlapping rib shoulders up to2.3mdeep and opaque northern wall closures. Glasspanes replace frame tiles;27sloping rafters meet a separate roofrim and raised rigidfoundation. Height40.5m is an estimate, not a published/surveyed total. North pavement and south lower entrance occupy different ground levels on an escarpment; this source uses a single low entrance footing y0 with no terrainbaked. Northgrade concealment and southern approach require laterappvalidation. No grounds, lawn, garage, logos, lettering orflag authored. Panel/rib dimensions are visual approximations, not fabrication drawings. Wall and glass facade tiles adjoin without overlays; near/far retain the distinguishing profile and separate window bays. Colours were calibrated to the checked exterior photographs. No terrain is baked.
